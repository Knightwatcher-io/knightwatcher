Free and open

All of it is free: the code under the AGPL-3.0, the art under CC BY-SA 4.0. There are no paid packs, no accounts and nothing behind a paywall.

Contributions of code and art are welcome: how to contribute.

Contributions of code and art are welcome, each commit signed off under the Developer Certificate of Origin: a Signed-off-by: line that certifies you wrote the change, or otherwise have the right to submit it under the project's licences.
