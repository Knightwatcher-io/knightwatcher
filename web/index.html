Notes for the reviewer

Everything shown is invented. No figure, name or log line comes from the homelab. The page has no connection to it.

The scene is the whole screen. The log and the bars are gone. A headline and one line of reason lie over the top only while something needs saying. When all is quiet the screen keeps the clock and one small line.

Active now takes several at once. The highest wins the scene, the knight's state and the headline, and a badge counts the warnings it outranks. The order: under attack, WAN down, unhealthy container, disk, backup, Pi silent, CPU, swap, then an SSH session, an update, a Plex stream, night, and last the quiet scenes.

Scenes. Quiet: the battlement, the camp in the woods, the house and the training yard take turns, and in each the knight strolls between a few spots and stands a while at each. Pinned: the king's court for an SSH session, the smithy for an update, the cellar for a full disk, the yard for a hot CPU, the battlement for an attack or a lost WAN, and home for the rest. The raspberry pie is the Raspberry Pi.

Movement is kept small. Fires flicker, the pie steams and the knight moves on every 12 to 22 seconds. The whole picture and the words also drift a few pixels over minutes, against burn-in.

Fun waits for serious. Each blocked advert adds a fly, up to eight. Outdoors and with nothing serious up, the knight swats them all at most once every 30 seconds. Swat now skips the wait. Events show a caption at the bottom for a few seconds.

Two grids for now. The knight, the battlement and the camp are drawn on a grid five times finer than before, 960 by 600. The house, yard, cellar, smithy and court still use the coarse grid until the finer look is agreed.

Sizes. The sign-maker's rule of 1 inch per 10 feet asks for 33 mm at 4 m and 25 mm at 3 m.
