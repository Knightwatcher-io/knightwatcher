Notes for the reviewer

Everything shown here is invented. This is the demo, which runs when the page has no state file. No figure, name or log line comes from a real machine.

The scene is the whole screen. The log and the bars are gone. A headline and one line of reason lie over the top only while something needs saying. When all is quiet the screen keeps the clock and one small line.

Active now takes several at once. The highest wins the scene, the knight's state and the headline, and a badge counts the warnings it outranks. The order: under attack, WAN down, unhealthy container, disk, backup, Pi silent, a watched thing down, CPU, swap, then an SSH session, an update, a Plex stream, night, and last the quiet scenes.

Scenes. Quiet: the battlement, the camp in the woods, the house and the training yard take turns, and in each the knight strolls between a few spots and stands a while at each. Pinned: the royal court for an SSH session, the smithy for an update, the cellar for a full disk, the yard for a hot CPU, the battlement for an attack or a lost WAN, and home for the rest. A watched thing down keeps the scene of the moment, and the knight looks towards its place. The raspberry pie is the Raspberry Pi. Other watched things are drawn as the devices they are, cameras high and the rest on the ground, each with a lamp that fades green while its machine is up and blinks red when it is down.

Movement is kept small. Fires flicker, the pie steams and the knight moves on every 12 to 22 seconds. The whole picture and the words also drift a few pixels over minutes, against burn-in.

Fun waits for serious. Each blocked advert adds a fly, up to eight. Outdoors and with nothing serious up, the knight swats them all at most once every 30 seconds. Swat now skips the wait. Events show a caption at the bottom for a few seconds.

One grid. The knight, every scene and every prop are drawn by code on a grid of 960 by 600, in whole pixels.

Sizes. The sign-maker's rule of 1 inch per 10 feet asks for 33 mm at 4 m and 25 mm at 3 m.

On the tablet. Full screen hides this bar and asks the browser to keep the screen awake. Tap the display to bring the bar back. The address keeps the chosen settings.
