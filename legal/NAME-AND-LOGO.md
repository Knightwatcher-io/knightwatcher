# The name and the logo

The name Knightwatcher and its logo are not under the code's licence (the AGPL) or the art's
(CC BY-SA 4.0). They are kept for the project, so that people can tell what comes from it. The
logo files are `web/logo.svg`, `web/logo-dark.svg` and `web/logo-mono.svg`, with the favicon and
the app icons made from it (`web/favicon.svg`, `web/favicon.ico`, `web/icon-192.png`,
`web/icon-512.png`, `web/apple-touch-icon.png` and the drawings in `tools/icons/`), and the same
files where the website uses them.

What that means in practice:

- Anyone may use the name to refer to the project: in an article, a video, a review, a list of
  homelab tools, a guide to installing it, or a pack made for it. The logo may be shown, unchanged,
  beside the same.
- A copy passed on unchanged, such as a clone, a mirror or a container image built from this
  repository, carries the name, the logo and the icons as they are.
- On your own install, change whatever you like.
- A fork kept to send changes back to the project may keep the name while it does.
- A fork that changes things and goes its own way uses a name and a logo of its own, so that
  nobody mistakes it for the project. It is welcome to say that it is based on Knightwatcher.

That is all this note asks. If you are unsure whether a use is fine, ask at
support@knightwatcher.io.
